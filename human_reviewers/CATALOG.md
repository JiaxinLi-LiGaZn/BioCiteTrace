# Human review source catalog

This catalog indexes the 17 review and process documents located in Google Drive on 14 September 2026. It includes eight independent reviewer records across scGPT, scVI, scGen and GEARS, five consensus/coordinator documents, one unfilled original reviewer form, and three result/process summaries. Fifteen newly archived documents have unedited DOCX and PDF exports; the two existing scGPT reviewer backups remain at their original paths.

The eight independent reviewer records contain 400 formal-review entries, representing 200 method-specific sampled cases assessed by two reviewers. They also contain 12 calibration-review entries for the three scGPT and three scVI calibration cases. These are counts of review entries and method-specific cases, not counts of unique publications across all four methods.

Source documents were discovered using method-name searches, reviewer/consensus/validation filename searches, citation/figure/sampling filename searches, and the relevant accessible folder listing. The catalog is complete for the review documents identified in that search; it does not assert that every production artifact exists in Drive. Drive links retain their existing access permissions. The repository exports can be read without access to the private source documents.

## Relationship to Figure 2

- The scVI consensus backup contains 50 human labels and marks 40 classification cases. The later consolidated report explains that HV-032 became a machine no-final abstention after the rescue update; the current figure uses the remaining 39 machine-classified cases. The earlier source wording is preserved, not silently rewritten.
- The scGen consensus document supplies aggregate TP/FP/FN counts, 37/40 exact primary-use agreement, and the three named mismatch cases. It also retains macro averages and literal zero-denominator conventions from that document. Figure 2 omits macro averages and shows both rates as N/A when there are no positive human-reference labels. These are different reporting conventions; the source table is not substituted for the figure table.
- The provisional scGPT document evaluates non-exclusive use labels. Figure 2 evaluates mutually exclusive primary categories. Its provisional metrics must not be substituted for Figure 2D.
- The historical GEARS rescue note ends at 40 classified studies. The final figure reports 84 classified studies; the historical note remains a dated process record.
- All existing figure files and aggregate CSV values were preserved during this archive update. The new documents were not used to manufacture missing machine predictions or to reconstruct rounded figures as exact counts.

## Remaining evidence gaps

1. A completed, frozen scGPT case-level consensus was not found. The located scGPT coordinator is entirely unfilled, although later summary documents refer to completed consensus artifacts elsewhere.
2. The located GEARS coordinator is only partially reconciled and has incomplete lock/consensus-status fields. This source snapshot cannot certify a fully frozen six-field reference.
3. The complete machine-to-human scoring crosswalks, per-case classification-versus-process-audit assignments for every method, final machine primary labels, and sampling probabilities were not found in the searched Drive review records. The scVI backup supplies its human-case scope flags, but not the paired machine predictions. The scGen document supplies aggregate counts and three mismatch descriptions, not a complete machine-label table.
4. The complete production citation snapshots, filter decisions, evidence capsules, LLM role outputs and adjudication traces are still absent. Source documents reference such artifacts, but references and fingerprints are not the artifacts themselves.

These gaps limit independent recomputation from the public package. They do not establish that the missing files do not exist on the original research system.
