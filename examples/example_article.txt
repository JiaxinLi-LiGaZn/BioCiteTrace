# Methods

We initialized the analysis encoder with CellMapFM [12] and fine-tuned it on a public peripheral-blood single-cell dataset collected before and after interferon stimulation.

# Results

CellMapFM [12] separated an interferon-responsive monocyte state and ranked IFITM3 and ISG15 among the genes most associated with that state. Targeted expression measurements in the source dataset supported the same response pattern.
