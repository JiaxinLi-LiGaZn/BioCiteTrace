The question

A citation is evidence of attention, not necessarily application

While reviewing AI methods in single-cell biology, we saw substantial citation counts for several influential models. Yet the biological analyses we encountered seemed less frequent than the headline numbers suggested.

That observation led to a more specific question: did a citing paper apply the method to answer a biological question, extend it, benchmark it, use it in another executed way, or only mention it?

BioCiteTrace turns that question into a study-level evidence review for scVI, scGPT, scGen and GEARS.

From citation to evidence

So we LLMs read the citing papers.

Two independent LLM assessments are consolidated from eligible study evidence, followed by blinded human review of evaluation samples.

Read the full workflow ↗

  1. Identify citing studies

    Link paper versions and deduplicate records so the counting unit is one scientific study.

  2. Verify eligible evidence

    Locate approved full text and retain explicit evidence-coverage states.

  3. Independent LLM review

    Two reviewers classify the study from the same evidence without seeing one another’s answers.

  4. Consolidate review outcomes

    Resolve supported decisions and keep genuinely unresolved cases out of result denominators.

  5. Sampled human validation

    Two blinded human reviewers establish consensus reference labels for an evaluation sample.

AI disclaimer — please read before the results

This website was built with AI assistance. LLM classifications may contain errors. Sampled human reviews are complete for all four methods.

LLMs produced the corpus-wide citation-use classifications. Human consensus was used as a sampled reference standard, not as absolute ground truth. The results are point estimates and do not yet include confidence intervals.

Thanks to Tibo for the resets that helped us run the LLM review pipeline across all four methods.

What the review found

The meaning of a citation differs markedly by method

Among studies with resolved primary-use classifications, 40.2% of scVI citations were biological applications and 16.4% were mention only. For scGPT, scGen and GEARS, mention only was the largest category, accounting for 70.9%, 73.4% and 61.9% of classified studies, respectively.

The human check

Agreement is strong in some categories and uneven in others

Precision asks how often a category assigned by the LLM reviewers agreed with the human reference. Recall asks how many human-reference positives the LLM review recovered. These are category-specific operating points from hard labels, not precision-recall curves.
